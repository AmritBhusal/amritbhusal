# CyberSource vs Other International Payment Gateways

CyberSource is a well-established international payment gateway owned by Visa, offering advanced fraud prevention, global payment support, and enterprise-level security. Here's how it compares to other major gateways like Stripe, PayPal, Adyen, and Authorize.Net.

## Pros of CyberSource

* **Strong Fraud Prevention** — Uses Visa's Decision Manager, one of the most advanced fraud detection tools.
* **Global Coverage** — Supports 190+ countries and 50+ currencies.
* **Multi-Payment Support** — Accepts credit cards, digital wallets such as Apple Pay and Google Pay, and alternative payment methods.
* **Enterprise-Level Security** — PCI DSS compliant with tokenization and 3D Secure.
* **Customizable API** — Provides robust APIs for integrations, similar to Stripe but more complex.
* **Omnichannel Payments** — Supports e-commerce, mobile, and in-person payments.

## Cons of CyberSource

* **Complex Setup** — More difficult to set up than Stripe or PayPal, requiring developer resources.
* **Pricing Not Transparent** — No public pricing, making cost comparison harder.
* **Support Issues** — Some users report slower customer support compared to Stripe's service.
* **Not Ideal for Small Businesses** — More focused on enterprise-level businesses, whereas Stripe and PayPal are more startup-friendly.

## Comparison with Other Gateways

| Payment Gateway | Best For                                            |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| CyberSource     | Enterprise businesses and advanced fraud prevention |
| Stripe          | Startups and developers                             |
| PayPal          | Small businesses and easy payment setup             |
| Adyen           | Global enterprises and omnichannel payments         |
| Authorize.Net   | Small businesses and traditional payment processing |

## Final Verdict

* **Best for Enterprises** → CyberSource & Adyen
* **Best for Startups & Developers** → Stripe
* **Best for Small Businesses** → PayPal & Authorize.Net
* **Best for Global Expansion** → Adyen & CyberSource
